Look through the model's cameras: right-click a camera node in the hierarchy and choose Preview camera to render the viewport from it (following its animation, masked to its authored frame). Scrolling zooms the preview; clicking the viewport or pressing ESC returns to the viewer camera; the UI stays usable while previewing. The details panel gains a Camera section for camera nodes (projection, field of view, aspect, clip planes and the live preview zoom).
